Four steps. From request to first visit.

There is no online calendar. You send a request, and the office schedules the visit with you.

  • 1. Choose your carePediatric dentistry, orthodontics or both. The form offers Ortho, Pedo or Both.
  • 2. Send the requestYour name, email, phone and an optional comment, on the practice's own form.
  • 3. Wait for the confirmationThe scheduling coordinator will contact you to confirm a time.
  • 4. Bring the patient formPrint and complete the form for your visit and bring your insurance card.

Two kinds of first visit. Pick the one that matches your appointment type.

{{pic:abbey-portrait}}

Pediatric first visit

The team reviews history, Dr. Abbey counts your child's teeth, and a cleaning, fluoride and X-rays follow if needed.

{{pic:jake-portrait}}

Orthodontic first visit

You meet the team, receive an exam with X-rays, and review a treatment plan and payment options with Dr. Jake.

Bring your insurance card to either visit. Cost depends on the treatment plan, and payment options are discussed before treatment starts.

How requests work

The practice's online form sends a request, not a booking. The scheduling coordinator contacts you, and your appointment is only set once the office confirms it.
