Four steps

From request to first visit

The practice's form is an appointment request. Your appointment is not confirmed until the office contacts you.

  1. Select your specialty

    Pediatric dentistry, orthodontics — or both (the practice's form offers Ortho, Pedo or Both).

  2. Send an appointment request

    Name, email, phone, appointment type and an optional comment on the practice's own form.

    Open the request form

  3. Receive confirmation from the office

    Our scheduling coordinator will contact you to confirm.

  4. Complete the patient forms

    Print and fill out the applicable form before your first appointment.

    Go to forms

What to expect

Pediatric Dentistry

Your first visit is all about your child

The team reviews your child's dental and medical history, Dr. Abbey gently “counts” their teeth, and children under 3 usually have a comforting “lap exam”. Cleaning, fluoride and digital X-rays are done if necessary, followed by an individual plan and time for your questions.

Orthodontics

Every smile starts with an initial consultation

Meet the team, learn about orthodontics, receive a complete initial exam, and find out which treatment options may suit you. The team reviews your history forms, performs an oral exam with X-rays, helps create a customised plan and discusses financial information and payment plans.

Request an orthodontic visit{{icon:phone}} {{ORTHO}}

Good to know

  • {{icon:calendar}}Visits during treatment are about every four to eight weeks.
  • {{icon:smile}}Regular dental checkups every six months continue during treatment.
