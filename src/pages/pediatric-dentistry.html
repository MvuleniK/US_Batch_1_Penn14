First pediatric dental visit

Your first visit is all about your child

Comfort, happiness and health come first. Here's what to expect, as described by the practice.

  1. We get to know your child

    The team reviews your child's dental and medical history first.

  2. A gentle exam

    Dr. Abbey “counts” your child's teeth and shows them her mirror. Children under 3 will likely have a “lap exam” — you hold and comfort your child, knee to knee with Dr. Abbey.

  3. Cleaning & check-up care

    After the exam, the team may clean and polish your child's teeth. Fluoride treatment is recommended at check-ups, and digital X-rays are taken if necessary.

  4. A plan, and your questions answered

    Dr. Abbey creates an individual treatment plan and answers any questions you have.
