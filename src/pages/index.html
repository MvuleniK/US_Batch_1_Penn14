When should my child first see a dentist?

The practice recommends booking as soon as your child gets a first tooth. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry advises a visit within six months of the first tooth appearing, or by age one, whichever comes first.

When is the right time for an orthodontic evaluation?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first visit around age seven. Orthodontic treatment isn't only for children and teens, and adults are welcome too.

How does booking an appointment work?

Use the practice's appointment request form (Ortho, Pedo or Both). It is a request, not an instant booking, and the scheduling coordinator will contact you to confirm. You can also call the office directly.
