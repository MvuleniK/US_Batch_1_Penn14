All FAQs {{icon:arrow}}
When should my child first see a dentist?

The practice recommends booking as soon as your child gets a first tooth. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry advises a visit within six months of the first tooth appearing, or by age one, whichever comes first.

{{src:site}} {{src:review}}

When is the right time for an orthodontic evaluation?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first visit around age seven. Orthodontic treatment isn't only for children and teens — adults are welcome too.

{{src:site}} {{src:review}}

How does booking an appointment work?

Use the practice's appointment request form (Ortho, Pedo or Both). It is a request, not an instant booking — the scheduling coordinator will contact you to confirm. You can also call the office directly.
