Three steps to your first visit

  1. Request your visit

    Fill in the practice's form with your name, email and phone, and choose Ortho, Pedo or Both.

  2. Get the visit confirmed

    The scheduling coordinator will contact you to confirm a time. Nothing is booked until then.

  3. Prepare for your visit

    Print the pediatric or orthodontic form from the practice's site and bring it with your insurance card.
