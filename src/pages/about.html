Pediatric dentistry

Dr. Abbey Sullivan Orozco, DMD

Dr. Abbey believes every child should get to experience a healthy smile. She has a unique ability to shape her young patients' views of dentistry, and to educate them about their oral health so they understand its importance as adults. She loves showing children that dentistry can be fun.

She attended the University of Notre Dame (Bachelor of Arts) and earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Temple University School of Dentistry. She completed her pediatric dental residency and certificate at Temple University Hospital/Episcopal Division. A native of Scranton, PA, she enjoys skiing, golfing and jogging along Kelly Drive.

Credentials and affiliations

  • Diplomate, American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, with annual recertification including continuing education
  • Member, American Dental Association
  • Member, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry
  • Omicron Kappa Upsilon (National Dental Honor Society), inducted 2007

Orthodontics

Dr. Jacob Orozco, DMD, MS

Dr. Jacob Orozco understands the confidence that comes with a beautiful smile. By combining cutting-edge technology with treatment plans tailored to each patient, he helps patients attain the smile they always wanted. Dr. Jake makes a point of getting to know his patients, and he loves hearing about their sports, music, vacations and proms.

He grew up in northwestern Pennsylvania, graduated from The Ohio State University, and received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Temple University School of Dentistry. He then completed post-doctoral specialty training in orthodontics and a master's degree in oral biology. When he's not fixing crooked teeth, he can often be found running, rowing or biking along Kelly Drive.

Credentials and affiliations

  • David C. Hamilton research award, Pennsylvania Association of Orthodontists
  • Member, American Dental Association
  • Member, American Association of Orthodontists
  • Member, Middle Atlantic Society of Orthodontists
  • Member, Greater Philadelphia Society of Orthodontists
  • The practice is a certified Invisalign Teen® provider
