  (children and adults appear in several) are covered by signed releases.
- Higher-resolution originals: the current site's files are small (600 px wide for the office tour, 428 px for portraits),
  so they look soft on large and high-density screens.
- Confirm the people shown are identified correctly in the alt text (matched to the practice's own labelled photos).
- The practice's logo exists on its site only as a photo of the wall sign; a clean logo file is needed.

Not used: the homepage slideshow (stock-style banners that may be licensed to the site's host), intraoral close-ups,
and personal snapshots.
