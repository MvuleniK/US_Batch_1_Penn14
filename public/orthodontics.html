Your first orthodontic visit

  1. Meet the team and have an exam

    You review your history forms and receive a complete oral exam with X-rays, to find out whether treatment is needed.

  2. Review your treatment plan

    Dr. Jake builds a plan with you and talks through financial information, insurance options and payment plans.

  3. Start orthodontic treatment

    Your first appointment places the appliance and takes about an hour. Visits then follow every four to eight weeks.
