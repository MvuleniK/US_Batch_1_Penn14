The orthodontic journey

What to expect at your first visit

Every smile starts with an initial consultation, as described by the practice.

  1. Request your consultation

    Send an appointment request (choose “Ortho”) or call the office. The scheduling coordinator will contact you to confirm.

  2. Meet the team & exam

    Review dental and medical history forms and receive a complete oral exam, with X-rays, to determine whether treatment is necessary.

  3. A customised plan

    Create a treatment plan with Dr. Jake, and discuss financial information, insurance options and payment plans.

  4. Appliance placement & visits

    Your first appointment places the appliance. Visits then happen about every four to eight weeks, scheduled around your life.

Orthodontic FAQs

All orthodontic FAQs
What's the best age to visit the orthodontist?

Any age can be a great time. The AAO recommends children first visit around age seven, and about one in five orthodontic patients is over 21.

From current website

Do braces hurt?

Braces do not often hurt, though you may feel a small amount of discomfort for a couple of days as your mouth gets used to them.
